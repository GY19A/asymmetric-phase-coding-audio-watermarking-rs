Test media
==========

The three clips in this directory are 10-second excerpts from the LibriSpeech
ASR corpus, test-clean subset:

  V. Panayotov, G. Chen, D. Povey, S. Khudanpur. "LibriSpeech: an ASR corpus
  based on public domain audio books." ICASSP 2015.
  https://www.openslr.org/12/

LibriSpeech is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International license (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
The underlying recordings are LibriVox public-domain audiobooks.

Changes: each utterance was cut or padded to 10.000 s, resampled to 44100 Hz,
mono, and stored as 16-bit PCM WAV. The files are unchanged copies of
